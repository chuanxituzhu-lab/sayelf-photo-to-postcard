# Photo-to-Postcard template workflows

This record distills the two supplied workflows into one local-first pipeline. The user photo remains the evidence anchor and every upload is exported as its own independent work.

## Shared pipeline

1. Read the current image locally and identify its subject, structure, pose, color relationships, light, depth and visual anchor.
2. Generate the four 03 copy fields from that evidence: Chinese title, English title, subtitle and postcard phrase. Keep the combination different from recent local history.
3. Extract three core colors and use them for the paper, typography, accents and lower artwork.
4. Choose a template workflow and an art medium. The template controls the lower design method; the art medium controls the visual treatment inside that method.
5. Keep the top original photo as evidence. Allow proportional scaling and gentle editorial grading, but do not replace, repaint, stretch, recolor or move the subject.
6. Generate one independent PNG per uploaded photo. Do not combine unrelated photos, add invented locations or publish the local source files.

## Distilled workflows

### Fused 3:4 / 50:50 default

The upper half is the original photograph. The lower half is a warm-paper concept poster that removes background noise and rebuilds only the most recognizable subject, silhouette, structure, pose and narrative relationship. Typography becomes the lower composition: the subject can sit inside letterforms, cross their boundaries, or extend along a typographic path. Use large positive and negative shapes, deliberate blank space, and restrained auxiliary text. This is the default fused workflow.

### Fixed information master

Use the established master with the source image above, the metadata and copy block at lower left, and the same-scene artistic translation at lower right. Keep the macro slots, paper, frame, information hierarchy and source identity fixed. Let only the lower art slot's medium, brushwork and internal whitespace rhythm change.

### Front and back display

Keep the original photograph in the upper evidence area. On warm ivory paper below, display two same-size physical postcards derived from the same photo: a front with a representative crop, title and short note; and a back with a center line, address rules, one subject-derived stamp and a restrained postmark. Use realistic paper thickness, shadows and fibers without turning the result into a product mockup.
